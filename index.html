The problem

Women are forced to choose between style and function.

Women's clothing has been designed around aesthetics — not around how women actually move through the world. The result? Fake pockets. Decorative seams. Invisible openings too small to hold a phone.

Every day, women carry bags not out of preference, but out of necessity — because their clothing simply doesn't hold what they need. This is a design failure, and it has real consequences: financial cost, physical burden, and a quiet message that women's ease of movement is less important than silhouette.

Function is already transforming how we dress — for athleisure, for menswear, for workwear. But it hasn't arrived yet for women's everyday clothing. That gap is the opportunity.

Our solution

How we're different

Engineered storage

Not just pockets — real carrying capacity

Deep, secure pockets designed to hold what you actually carry: phone, cards, keys, lip gloss — without bulk or discomfort. Pocket depth and placement engineered from the ground up.

Invisible functionality

Polished look, hidden utility

Functional storage integrated into tailored silhouettes — so your outfit stays sharp whether you're in a boardroom or heading to happy hour. No cargo aesthetics required.

Female-first design

Built for women's bodies, not scaled-down menswear

Pockets sit correctly, distribute weight naturally, and don't sag or pull. Designed around how women's bodies actually move — not an afterthought on a men's template.

Founder

Built by the customer

Meenakshi A. Bhatia
CEO + Founder · Philadelphia, USA
Wharton MBA Data science Entrepreneurship

I started MAB Untethered after a simple moment at work: I was heading into a meeting, juggling my laptop, notebook, and coffee — and instinctively tried to slip my phone into my pocket, only to realize I didn't have one. Meanwhile, my male colleagues were completely hands-free.

That moment wasn't small. It was a reminder of something bigger: women's clothing still isn't designed for how we actually move through the world. I'm building MAB Untethered as the customer. I live this problem every day. I've altered my own clothes to fit my daily function.

My operator background: I've built my career identifying broken systems and rebuilding them — from data science to entrepreneurship to leading women's initiatives at Wharton. I spot inefficiencies, test quickly, and build systems that scale. MAB Untethered is a business of iteration, and I know how to run that loop.

The edge: combining lived experience with data-driven execution to redefine functionality as a standard in women's clothing — not a niche feature.
