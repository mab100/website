The Night-Out Jean

$73

High quality, tailored denim that actually holds your phone, card, keys, lip-gloss — everything you need on a night out. You can leave your bag at home.

Select Size
Details
Wide-leg, high-rise denim. Two deep front pockets — sized to fit a full phone, keys, cards, and more. Back patch pockets. Button and zip fly closure.
Material
98% Cotton, 2% Elastane. Mid-weight denim with a slight stretch for comfort and movement. Washed for a lived-in, effortless look.
Care
Machine wash cold, inside out. Tumble dry low or hang to dry. Do not bleach. Iron inside out on low if needed.
Fit & Sizing
Model is 5'8" and wearing a size S. Runs true to size with a relaxed fit through the hip and thigh, and a straight wide leg from knee down.
