The problem

Women are forced to choose between style and function.

Women's clothing has been designed around aesthetics, and not around how women actually move through the world. The result? Fake pockets. Decorative seams. Invisible openings too small to hold a phone.

Every day, women carry bags out of necessity, because our clothing simply doesn't hold what we need. This is a design failure, and it has real consequences: financial cost, physical burden, and a quiet message that women's ease of movement is less important than silhouette.

Function is already transforming how we dress - for athleisure, for menswear, for workwear. But it hasn't arrived yet for women's everyday clothing. That gap is the opportunity.

"If I could find a pair of cute, well-tailored dress pants that actually fit my phone, I'd buy three pairs immediately."
