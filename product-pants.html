The Untethered Pant

$158

Wide-leg dress pants with deep, phone-ready pockets. Tailored for the boardroom, functional enough for real life. Engineered so you never have to choose between looking sharp and moving freely.

Select Size
Details
Wide-leg silhouette with front pleats. High-rise waist with belt loops. Two deep front pockets — large enough for a full-size phone. Two back welt pockets. Front zip and button closure.
Material
82% Modal, 12% Polyester, 6% Elastane. Lightweight, wrinkle-resistant, and structured without being stiff. Machine washable.
Care
Machine wash cold on gentle cycle. Lay flat to dry or hang. Do not bleach. Iron on low if needed.
Fit & Sizing
Model is 5'9" and wearing a size S. These pants run true to size with a relaxed fit through the hip and a full wide leg.
